# My agent: SubTrack & Budget Copilot
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps individuals manage recurring subscriptions and optimize monthly budgets with a catalog of tracked expenses and financial recommendations.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User's monthly income, savings targets, financial preferences (e.g., frugal vs. convenience-focused), and active subscription services.
- Tools: Look up active subscriptions, log new recurring expenses, calculate projected annual costs, and simulate compound interest or savings growth.
- Catalog/UI: Subscription cards (service name, billing cycle, renewal date, cost, cancellation difficulty), budget category breakdown table.
- Image gen: Personalized financial milestone badges and achievement celebration visuals (e.g., "Saved $500 milestone achieved!").
- Sandbox: Compound savings interest simulation, currency conversion, and annual subscription cost forecasting.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for financial projections and compound savings calculations, Firestore for subscription catalog storage.
